01 / OUR MISSION

A space to connect, experiment and grow.

Emergence Arts Forum is a community-oriented space for cultural and creative practitioners. We aim to encourage collaboration, create opportunities for exchange and make creative communities stronger.

Through gatherings, discussions, shared resources and creative projects, EAF seeks to connect people who might otherwise be working alone.

The forum is being developed with its community. Our digital platform is a first step toward making communication and coordination easier.

03 / OUR PARENT INITIATIVE
